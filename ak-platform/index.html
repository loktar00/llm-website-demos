History

Kalashnikov, 1947

Mikhail Kalashnikov began the design in 1945. His rifle went through the Soviet trials of 1946 and 1947 and was adopted by the Soviet Army in early 1949 as the Avtomat Kalashnikova.

The first stamped receivers proved hard to make, so production moved to machined (milled) receivers: the Type 2 and the lighter Type 3. In 1959 the stamped, lighter AKM replaced it, and in 1974 the AK-74 moved the family to the smaller 5.45×39 mm round.

Simple to make and known for its reliability in mud, sand and cold, it became the most produced rifle in history: about 100 million Kalashnikov-pattern rifles, some 75 million of them AK-47s. It appears on the flag of Mozambique.
