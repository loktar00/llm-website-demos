Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Domestic flat rate $6 — free on bundles of $35 or more.
  • 2–5 business days standard, dispatched from our studio by 2pm.
  • International $14 flat to 40+ countries, with tracking.
  • Everything ships in recycled kraft with zero single-use plastic.
30 days

Returns

  • 30 days, no quiz. If it didn’t make you smile, send it back.
  • Unopened goods for a full refund; opened for store credit.
  • Prepaid label emailed within a day — just drop it in any mailbox.
  • Missing or damaged item? We re-send it free, first ask.
