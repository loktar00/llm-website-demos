02 — The Router

A gate decides who wakes up.

Every layer holds a ring of experts. For each token a router lights just two of them and leaves the rest dark and still — sparse activation is why a model this large can answer this quickly.

8 experts per layer · 2 active per token — illustrative. Hover a lit chamber to see its specialism.

03 — The Experts

Huge knowledge. Small footsteps.

Inside a lit chamber your fragment is transformed — sharpened, coloured, given detail. Thousands of tokens take different paths at the same moment, so the whole model runs with the weight of a small one.

Architecture and routing behaviour on this page are illustrative.

04 — Drafting Ahead

It writes ahead, then checks its work.

Several candidate tokens are drafted in parallel at every step. A verification wave accepts the good ones — snap — and shatters the rest. More accepted tokens per step is more speed.

Multi-token prediction shown as a fan of six candidates. Illustrative.
