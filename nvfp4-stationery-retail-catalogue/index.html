Shipping & Returns

the boring-but-nice part

We ship fast & flat

Every order leaves our little workshop within 1 business day, in recycled boxes with (free) gift wrap. US standard: 2–4 days, free over $35. Tracking email the moment it posts.

30-day, no-fuss returns

Changed your mind? Bring anything back within 30 days for a full refund — no questions, no return shipping in the US. Damaged in transit? We replace it for free, same day.

Small print, big heart

International shipping at flat rates (from $9). Custom or wholesale orders: give us 5 business days. And yes — the ink on your order confirmation is, in fact, Midnight Berry.
