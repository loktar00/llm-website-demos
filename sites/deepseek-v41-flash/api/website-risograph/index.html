Chapter One — What is Ink Weather?

A fair for people who smell like ink.

Ink Weather is a three-day festival celebrating independent print culture on the West Coast — the smudged, the misregistered, the passionately over-inked.

Expect working presses, live hot-foil jams, tables stacked with zines, and the gentle hiss of a hundred Riso drums turning at once. Bring your sketchbook. Bring your hands. Bring old clothes.

All ages. Sliding-scale tickets. No gatekeeping, no glossy catalogues, no stock imagery.

Chapter Two — Spot Colour Separations

Four inks, one lucky accident.

Risograph prints one colour at a time. As the drum turns, each pass drifts a millimetre off — and that imperfection is the whole point. Watch the layers cycle.

Chapter Five — Getting around

A whole pier, mostly indoors.

Pier 47 is a converted warehouse on the Oakland Estuary. Five halls, four stages, one very long hallway of zines. Here's the lay of the land.

  • 1

    Hall A — Main Market

    Sixty printer tables, zines, and the front desk. Entrance off the loading dock.

  • 2

    Hall B — Talks & Screenings

    Seats 300. All talks free with a fair pass. Live captioned.

  • 3

    Studio Stage — Workshops

    Hands-on workshops and demonstrations. Aprons provided.

  • 4

    Press Room — Working Studio

    Four Riso drums, two letterpresses, and a very loud guillotine.

  • 5

    Café — Zine Swap & Snacks

    Pastries, filter coffee, and a swap shelf that never empties.

Chapter Eight — Frequently asked

Small print, big answers.

Can't find what you need? Drop us a line at hello@inkweather.fair — a real human will reply.

When and where is Ink Weather 2026?

Friday 27 – Sunday 29 September 2026 at Pier 47, 47 Embarcadero West, Oakland, CA. Doors open at 10am each day; Sunday closes with a toast at 6pm.

Do I need a ticket for every day?

Market entry is free all weekend. A $40 festival pass covers talks, workshops, and the Friday studio crawl (workshop materials included). Sliding-scale and pay-what-you-can passes are available — no questions asked.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. Pier 47 is step-free throughout, with accessible restrooms, a quiet room, and live captions for all talks. If you need anything specific, email us and we'll make it happen.

Can I sell my work at the fair?

Applications for printer tables open in March 2026. We reserve 20% of tables for first-time and student printers. Table fee is $120 for the weekend, with two free passes included.

Is this family-friendly?

Very. Sunday afternoon is Family Hour with stamps, stickers, and safe non-toxic inks. Kids under 12 get in free and usually leave with ink somewhere unexpected.

What should I bring?

An apron or old shirt, a tote bag for zines, your sketchbook, and a couple of prints for the swap shelf. We'll provide gloves, ink, and plenty of wipes.

Will the posters be available online after the fair?

Only leftovers. All editions are printed during the weekend, so if you want a specific run, come early — most sold out on Saturday last year.
