01Programme

Two days, seven rooms, one crooked register.

Talks on the stage, ink to the elbows in the Press Room, and the Machine Hall running hot all weekend. Every slot leaves with something printed in it.

02Ink Lab

Pull the plates out of register.

Three spot-colour plates, one sheet of paper. Drag a plate out of alignment and watch the halftone screens weave; snap them back and the image clicks into place — almost.

Live separation — CMYK's cooler sibling

03The roster

Six hands you'll ink up with.

The residents run the presses, teach the workshops and argue loudly about registration at the bar. Proof portraits pulled in two passes, on purpose.

04The limited wall

Six fair posters, pulled on-site.

Every poster below is an edition of fifty (Roller Disco: thirty), printed during the fair itself. Add to your stack now, pay at the door — when the drum runs out, the edition is done.

05The venue

Het Papiervat — a former paper warehouse.

Follow your nose: fresh toner-oil on the wind means you're close. Follow the coral dashes if your nose is broken.

07FAQ

Fine print, generously set.

Yes — all drums run vegetable-oil inks that clean up with soap and water. Your hands will be colourful for a day or two; consider it a souvenir. Tables, however, are not skin, and ink there is permanent by law of riso.

Fully step-free, wide doors throughout, and seating in every room. Sunday opens with a quiet hour (10–11, no music, half the drums). Large-print and high-contrast programmes are at the front desk, and ear defenders hang by the stage.

Yes — a half-table and a stool costs €10 for the weekend, first come at the door. One rule: everything on your table must have passed through a risograph at some point in its life.

Weekend-pass holders can reserve workshop seats online after purchase. A handful of seats are held for the door each morning — arrive when we open and put your name on the sheet like it's 1998.

A tote (you will acquire paper), clothes you can sacrifice to the yellow drum, and cash for the coffee bike. We provide gloves, aprons, and strong opinions about registration.

Full refund up to two weeks before the fair. After that, passes are transferable — print a friend's name on the back, in any ink, in any register.
