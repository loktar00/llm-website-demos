No. VII — Service Rituals

Four Things We Do Every Night

  1. I.

    The Welcome Aperitif

    Champagne is poured on the platform as the porters take your cases. The city begins to slide away before the first glass is finished, and conversation adjusts itself to the rhythm of the rails.

  2. II.

    The Turn-down

    While you are at dinner the berth is made in crisp ivory linen, a hot-water bottle is tucked beneath the pillow, and tomorrow's weather card is set on the ledge — written by hand, mostly correctly.

  3. III.

    The Sunrise Calling

    A soft knock and the curtain drawn back a hand's width. The lighthouses are behind you and the light on the water is, we find, worth the interruption. Coffee follows whether you rise or not.

  4. IV.

    The Last Look

    In the final miles the barman pours something short and local. Nobody speaks much. Port Meridian's arcades rise out of the morning haze, and the fish auction is already shouting.
