The Proposition

One Night Between Two Seas

The Aurelian Express is a hotel that happens to move. Eighty-four guests, nine carriages, and a coastline that does the entertaining: the Côte d'Azur at dinner, the Ligurian cliffs by nightlight, the Tyrrhenian at first coffee, the lagoon by late breakfast.

Everything aboard is unhurried on purpose. The train was built for people who have somewhere wonderful to be in the morning — and no interest whatsoever in arriving tired.

    Service Rituals

    Small Ceremonies, Kept Strictly

    1. I

      The Brass Key

      A solid brass key on a grosgrain ribbon — car, number, and the Aurelian sun. Yours until Venice; many ask to keep it. Some are allowed.

    2. II

      The First Pour

      Champagne while the harbour is still in view. The glass is chilled; the timing is not negotiable.

    3. III

      The Folding of the Berth

      While you are at dinner the steward remakes the room: sofa to bed, day to night, in eleven unhurried minutes.

    4. IV

      Lights to Blue

      At a signal from the wire, cabin lamps fall to a low harbour-blue. The corridor carpet glows the whole length of the train.

    5. V

      Coffee at First Light

      Brought at the exact minute you named the night before — with the day's route card and one perfect sfogliatella.
