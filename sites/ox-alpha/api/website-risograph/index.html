07 — Questions

Fair FAQ

Do I need printing experience to attend?

Not at all. Half our attendees have never touched a risograph. Workshops are beginner-friendly and every table has someone happy to explain why the ink smells so good.

Are the workshops included in my pass?

Entry to the fair is included. Workshops have small material fees ($8–$12) to cover paper and ink, paid at sign-up. Sign-up sheets post daily at 10:45 at the info desk.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — step-free entry via the 4th Ave loading dock, accessible restrooms on every floor, and seating available in all talk rooms. Email us ahead if you need anything specific and we'll sort it.

Can I sell my own prints there?

Studio tables are juried and applications open each January. But everyone can bring up to ten zines for the free community zine rack — no application needed.

What happens to sold-out posters?

They're gone! That's the point of a limited edition. Some printers do a second "misfit run" of imperfect prints sold cheap on Sunday — follow the ink smell.

Is it kid-friendly?

Very. Kids under 12 get in free, and the family print table runs all weekend with aprons in every size.
