06 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU
GET INKY.

First time? You’re in good company.
Here’s the stuff you might be wondering.

Do I need to know anything about printing?

Not a thing. This is a festival for the curious, whether you run your own studio or have never heard of a risograph. Our beginner workshops start from the very beginning, and the printers love a good question.

What’s included in my weekend ticket?

Your £12 pass gets you into the print fair on both days, all talks, the collaborative print, and the Sunday print swap. Workshops are included too, but have limited places: save your favourites in the programme and sign up at the welcome desk. Prints, food, and drinks are extra.

Can I bring the kids?

Absolutely. Under 16s come free with a ticketed adult. Our all-day making table and collaborative print welcome all ages. Machine-based workshops are suitable for ages 12 and up with adult supervision.

Is the venue accessible?

The entrance and all festival spaces are on one level, with step-free access, accessible toilets, and seating throughout. The talk corner has a hearing loop. A quiet room is available beside the welcome desk, and assistance dogs are welcome.

How do I get there, and what should I bring?

Enter from Paper Lane, beside the blue doors. There’s bike parking in the courtyard and the venue plan above shows all festival spaces. Bring a tote for your finds, clothes that won’t mind a spot of ink, and a reusable cup. All workshop tools and materials are provided. This is a fictional festival and address, so please don’t plan real travel.

What exactly is risograph printing?

Think of a photocopier with the soul of a screen-printing press. Each colour gets its own stencil and drum of ink. The colours layer, overlap, and land a tiny bit differently every time. The result is wonderfully bright, beautifully imperfect, and impossible to mistake for anything else.
