Risograph prints one spot colour at a time. Paper runs through, the drum rolls, translucent soy inks overprint. Misalignment? That's the magic. Toggle the separations, drag the wobble, watch it happen.

All on one level, all step-free. Follow the coral arrows from Bristol Temple Meads (12 min walk). Tap a building on the map!

HALL A — PRINT HALL
48 exhibitor tables, the big shop till, and the legendary free misprint bin. Loudest room, best smells (paper + coffee).

♿ Access — step-free throughout

Level access, accessible loos in Hall A + Garden, BSL on main talks, quiet room in Ink Lab. Blue badge parking on Fog Lane.

◉ Getting here

Temple Meads 12 min walk • Buses 1/2 stop outside • Bike racks in yard • No car park — please walk, wheel or bus!

◎ Food & drink

Taco Rosa, Small Goods coffee, Lovely Bread + natural wine bar Sat night. Free water + oat milk everywhere.

✦ Top tip from regulars

Bring a poster tube! Or buy our £3 recycled tube. Sunday 4pm is haggle o'clock — printers discount rather than carry stock home.
