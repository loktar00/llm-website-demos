01
№ 01 — THE INSTRUMENT

Four apps won't tell you whether to bother.

Cloud maps, moon calendars, planetariums, mount planners — Noctuary reads all of it so you don't assemble a control panel in a cold driveway at 11 p.m.

F·01

The Go / Don't-Go Score

Hour-by-hour blend of seeing (arcsec), transparency, cirrus, wind, dew point and moon separation, collapsed into a single 0–10 score for your horizon. We weight it for aperture, focal length and whether you're on Bortle 2 or Bortle 8.

F·02

Moon-aware target lists

No more suggesting M33 to someone under a 92% moon. Every target is ranked by contrast against tonight's actual lunar separation, sky brightness and your light-pollution map — narrowbands get a pass under bright skies.

F·03

Your rig, modeled

Log your scopes, mounts, focal reducers and eyepieces once. Noctuary computes max useful exposure without guiding, meridian-flip timing and balance limits before you unpack a single case.

F·04

Dew-curve alarms

Forecast dew point vs. ambient tells you when optics will fog — not when the clouds arrive. One notification: “heaters on by 23:10.” Sleep through the rest.

F·05

The session logbook

One-tap capture of notes: seeing at the eyepiece, gear, sketches, subs. Export as a printable almanac page — because paper still survives a -15°C night.

F·06

Dark-sky trip radar

Scans 160,000 candidate sites within range: SQM estimates, drive time, light-domes on the horizon, and cell coverage for the inevitable “is it still clear?” debate.
