06

SMALL PRINT

(but large curiosity)
Do I need a ticket?

Entry is free with a £5 optional wristband that supports the print fund. Workshops are free but capped — grab a place at the Courtyard desk from 9:30 each morning.

Can I bring my own work to print?

Yes! The Demo Shop runs open drum sessions (10:00–17:00). Bring black-and-white masters on A3 max, or bring artwork on a USB stick — Hana will happily rasterise your soul into a halftone.

Is it accessible?

Step-free throughout, reserved seating at talks, BSL interpreters on the stage programme, quiet room in the Kiln Row annex, and the yard has firm, level ground. Questions? email access@inkweather.fest — a human who smells of ink will reply.

Will my clothes survive?

Fluorescent coral does not come out. This is a feature. Wear something you’d happily declare “vintage, from Ink Weather” in 2035. Aprons provided for workshops.

Kids?

Very welcome. Sticky-Finger Sessions on Sunday are built for small hands, and the Stamp Trail runs all weekend with a prize of three limited prints and a sticker of questionable design.

Can I sell or table?

Tables sold out, but two cancellations are handled by lottery at the gate on Friday, and the Swap Table always accepts duplicates. Come play.
