THE DINING CAR

SUPPER AT 120 KILOMETRES AN HOUR

Under stepped chandeliers of seamed glass, service begins the moment the mainland falls away. Oysters opened at Valmora, butter churned at Cieldor, and a dessert named for the last city you'll wake beneath. The string trio retires when the lighthouses take over.
