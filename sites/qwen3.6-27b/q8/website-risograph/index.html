INK WEATHER celebrates the beauty of imperfect registration, the warmth of spot-color inks, and the community that gathers around the press. For two days, our studio fills with the smell of ink and the sound of rollers turning.

Whether you're a seasoned printer or picking up a squeegee for the first time, there's a place for you here.

Featured Artists

The Printers

Six makers pushing the boundaries of ink and paper.

Maya Chen
Risograph Alchemist

Maya runs Ghost Frequency Press out of a converted warehouse in Bushwick. Her risograph zines explore urban folklore and the hidden histories of city neighborhoods.

Risograph Zines Editorial
Leo Okonkwo
Letterpress Poet

Leo's letterpress work blends poetry and typography into tactile objects. His small press, Slow Type Studio, has published over 200 limited-edition broadsides since 2018.

Letterpress Typography Poetry
Sam Rivera
Screenprint Storyteller

Sam's hand-pulled screenprints feature bold, narrative illustrations drawn from family history and immigrant experiences. Their studio, Tinta Colectiva, hosts weekly print nights.

Screenprint Illustration Community
Aisha Patel
Zine Architect

Aisha builds elaborate zine structures that fold, unfold, and surprise. Her "Paper Palaces" series has been exhibited in galleries across three continents. She teaches zine workshops year-round.

Zines Bookbinding Collage
Jun Park
Lithography Dreamer

Jun's stone lithographs capture the liminal spaces between memory and landscape. Working at Moonstone Press, she mentors emerging artists in the ancient art of hand-pulled stone printing.

Lithography Landscape Mentorship
Rosa Martinez
DIY Press Pioneer

Rosa built her entire print studio from salvaged materials and teaches others to do the same. Her "Garage Press" workshops have launched dozens of independent print practices across the tri-state area.

DIY Education Mixed Media
Find Us

The Venue

A converted textile factory in the heart of Brooklyn's printing district.

The Print House Collective

A sprawling 15,000 sq ft converted textile factory on Paper Mill Road, our venue has been home to independent printers since 1987. Exposed brick, skylights, and the ever-present scent of ink make it the perfect setting for INK WEATHER.

  • September 13–14, 2025 · 9 AM – 9 PM
  • 420 Paper Mill Road, Brooklyn, NY 11237
  • Nearest subway: L train to Myrtle-Wyckoff
  • Wheelchair accessible · Gender-neutral restrooms
  • Parking available on Paper Mill & surrounding streets

FAQ
