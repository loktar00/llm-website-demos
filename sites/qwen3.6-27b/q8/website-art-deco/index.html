Along the Coast

The Seven Destinations

Port Aurelia

The Grand Departure

Where the Aurelian Express begins its legendary journey. A city of marble colonnades and golden harbours, where the sea meets the sky in an eternal embrace.

Silverhaven

The Mountain Pass

Nestled between ancient peaks and a mirror-like fjord, Silverhaven rewards travellers with silver-pine forests and thermal springs of legendary restorative power.

Côte d'Or

The Golden Coast

A landscape of sun-drenched vineyards and terraced hillsides, where centuries of viticulture have produced wines of unparalleled character and depth.

Marenova

The Azure Archipelago

A string of emerald islands connected by causeways of white stone, where turquoise waters lap against ancient sea caves and hidden coves.

Bella Marina

The Garden Harbour

A city of cascading terraces and botanical gardens, where every balcony overlooks a harbour filled with galleons and the scent of jasmine hangs in the air.

Azure Point

The Dramatic Headland

Where the coastline plunges into the sea in dramatic cliffs, Azure Point offers the most breathtaking vista on the entire route—a place of wild beauty and solitude.

Coralis

The Reef Kingdom

A city built upon living coral, where architecture and nature intertwine. The Coralis reefs glow with bioluminescence after dark—a spectacle unlike any other.

Solmar

The Final Destination

The journey's magnificent conclusion. Solmar's grand terminal, bathed in the first light of dawn, welcomes travellers with a ceremony of champagne and orchestral music.

The Aurelian Way

Service Rituals

I

The Luggage Ceremony

Each guest's luggage is met at the platform by a uniformed porter, stamped with a hand-carved brass medallion, and delivered to your suite within minutes of boarding.

II

The Welcome Elixir

A bespoke aperitif, crafted by our master mixologist to your preference, awaits in your suite upon arrival—served in hand-blown crystal with a sprig of coastal rosemary.

III

The Turndown Ritual

Each evening, your suite is prepared for rest with warm towels, a chamomile compress, and the gentle scent of sandalwood. The windows are drawn and the lamps dimmed to moonlight.

IV

The Morning Pressing

Leave your garments on the valet stand and they will be returned pressed and refreshed before breakfast—a tradition unchanged since 1924.

V

The Arrival Toast

As the train enters the final station, a string quartet plays in the lounge and champagne is poured. The conductor personally wishes each guest farewell.

VI

The Journey Journal

Each guest receives a leather-bound journal documenting the route, the cuisine, and the stories of the seven cities. It is yours to keep as a memento.
