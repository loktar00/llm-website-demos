Featured Artists

Six Printers

Meet the makers leading workshops and exhibiting work at INK WEATHER 2025.

Maya Chen

Risograph Alchemist

London-based printmaker whose colour-separation experiments blur the line between mistake and masterpiece. Her "Drift" series explores accidental registration as artistic language.

Tomás Reyes

Screen Printing Collective Lead

Runs a community screen-printing studio in Glasgow. Known for bold, layered posters that layer five to seven colours on heavy-weight paper stock.

Aisha Patel

Letterpress & Type

Craftsman printer specialising in hand-set letterpress. Her studio in Bristol houses a 1920s Chandler & Price press and over 200 type fonts.

Felix Okafor

Zine Culture Archivist

Publisher of "Underground Press" zine since 2016. Felix's workshops teach radical self-publishing and distribution networks for independent voices.

Lena Müller

Collage & Hand-Cut Illustration

Berlin-born, Manchester-based illustrator whose intricate hand-cut paper collages have been exhibited at the V&A and printed as limited editions.

Samira Khan

Poster Design & Colour Systems

Graphic designer turned full-time printer. Samira's colour-system posters explore Pantone alternatives through custom risograph ink mixing.

Common Questions

FAQ

How much are tickets?

+

Single day tickets are £18. A three-day pass is £45 (saving you £9). Workshops cost an additional £25–£45 each and include all materials. Under 18s enter free with an accompanying adult. Students with valid ID get 50% off.

Do I need experience to attend workshops?

+

Not at all! Our workshops are designed for all levels. Riso 101 and Screen Printing: From Shirt to Poster are perfect for beginners. Advanced workshops like Advanced Riso assume some prior knowledge. Each listing notes the skill level.

Can I buy posters online before the fair?

+

Yes! Our online shop ships UK-wide. Posters purchased online are shipped in custom archival tubes. International shipping is available with a flat £12 rate. Each poster is printed-to-order using the same risograph presses featured at the fair.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

+

Absolutely. The Printworks has a step-free entrance from Deansgate, accessible restrooms on all floors, and an elevator connecting all three levels. We also have a dedicated quiet room for attendees who need a break from the sensory experience. Please let us know if you need any additional accommodations.

Can I bring my own equipment?

+

You're welcome to bring personal items like sketchbooks, cameras, or printing tools. However, all workshop equipment is provided. If you're an exhibiting printer with your own press, please contact us about our bring-your-own-press programme.

Will there be food and drink?

+

Yes! Our on-site café serves coffee, tea, hot chocolate, and light meals throughout the day. We also have a dedicated bar that opens at 5pm each day (and stays open late on Saturday for the print party). A selection of vegan and gluten-free options are always available.
