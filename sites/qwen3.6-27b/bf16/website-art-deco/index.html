Port of Call

Seven Grand Destinations

Aurelia Bay

34°08'N 118°24'W

Where the journey begins among terraced gardens and a luminous harbor lit by a thousand lanterns.

Meridian Coast

33°45'N 117°58'W

Art Deco grandeur meets endless surf. The grand casino and oceanfront promenade define this jewel.

Sapphire Shores

33°12'N 117°30'W

Turquoise waters lap against white marble terraces. A haven of artists, poets, and midnight swimmers.

Coral Crescent

32°40'N 117°05'W

A sweeping bay lined with pastel villas and a legendary seaside market at golden hour.

Pearl Haven

32°10'N 116°40'W

The pearl divers' capital, where opulence is measured in strands and the harbormaster plays Chopin.

Jade Point

31°42'N 116°15'W

Emerald hills cascade to the sea. Famous for its cliffside observatory and the annual lantern festival.

Obsidian Cliffs

31°15'N 115°50'W

The final destination: volcanic cliffs, black sand beaches, and the grand terminus palace overlooking the horizon.
