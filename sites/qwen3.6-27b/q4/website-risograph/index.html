INK WEATHER PRINT FAIR & RISO FESTIVAL

Three days of risograph madness, screen-printing workshops, zine swaps, and the loudest ink rollers in Europe. Bring your hands, leave with ink.

The annual gathering of ink-obsessed makers

01 — What's On

Workshops & Talks

Workshop SAT 12 · 10:00–13:00

Riso 101: Your First Pull

Learn the basics of risograph operation—ink mixing, drum prep, and running your first sheet. Machines provided.

Talk SAT 12 · 15:00–16:30

The Misregistration Manifesto

Why offset errors are the soul of risograph. A 90-minute love letter to imperfect printing.

Workshop SUN 13 · 11:00–14:00

Halftone & Tone: Dots That Sing

Create custom halftone screens and learn how dot density becomes your palette.

Talk SUN 13 · 17:00–18:00

Print Culture in the Digital Age

A panel on why physical print is having its most vibrant renaissance since the 1980s.

Workshop MON 14 · 10:00–12:00

Collage & Cut: Layered Zines

Hand-cut techniques for building dense, layered zine layouts with riso-printed elements.

Panel MON 14 · 14:00–15:30

Independent Presses: Survival Guide

How six small studios keep the presses rolling in an algorithm-driven world.

02 — The Makers

Six Printers, Six Voices

MK

Maren Kessler

Riso Alchemist · Berlin

Runs "Tint & Grain," a studio where every print is a weather report. Known for 12-color layered posters that look like storms.

JT

Jun Tanaka

Screen Printer · Tokyo/Berlin

Brings Japanese linocut sensibility to European riso. His halftone portraits have a ghostly, breathing quality.

AO

Amara Osei

Zine Publisher · Accra

Founder of "Ground Press," publishing radical Afrofuturist zines on reclaimed paper. Prints with pigment made from local minerals.

LS

Léa Sauvage

Letterpress & Riso · Lyon

Hybrid printer who layers letterpress debosses under riso washes. Her textures are so tactile you'll want to lick the page.

DV

Dmitri Volkov

Experimental Printer · Minsk

Pushes riso machines past their limits—overprinting 20+ colors, intentional jamming, and "happy accident" editions.

CP

Carmen Prieto

Edition Printer · Mexico City

Specializes in limited-edition art prints. Her sunflower-yellow series has sold out in three continents.
