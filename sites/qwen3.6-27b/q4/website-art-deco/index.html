The Seven Ports of Call

Destinations of Legend

Veridian Haven

The Emerald Gateway

Where the Aurelian Express begins its journey beneath a canopy of ancient cypress, the harbor glows with bioluminescent waters.

Lumen Point

City of Perpetual Light

Terraced gardens cascade down luminous cliffs where prismatic glass towers catch the last amber rays of the setting sun.

Cassandra Bay

The Whispering Fortress

A medieval citadel perched above turquoise coves, where salt-wind carries the echoes of troubadour songs through arched galleries.

Aurelia del Mar

The Gilded Heart

The jewel of the coast—marble colonnades, golden-domed baths, and a promenade where the sea meets sky in one unbroken line.

Thalassa

The Deep Blue City

Built upon natural sea arches, Thalassa's submerged grottos glow with ancient phosphorescence beneath vaulted stone ceilings.

Oceana Tower

The Skyward Beacon

A singular monolith of reinforced glass and brass rises from a cliff-top plateau, its observation deck offering the coast's highest vista.

Meridian Crest

The Final Summit

The journey's terminus—a terraced city crowned by the Meridian Observatory, where the Aurelian Express meets the first light of dawn.

The Aurelian Way

Service Rituals

I

The Brass Polishing

Each morning, the attendants polish every brass fixture by hand—a tradition unchanged since 1934. The gleam is a promise: nothing aboard is overlooked.

II

The Evening Bell

At precisely 19:00, a single brass bell tolls from the locomotive's cab. It signals the transition from day to evening service, and the dining saloon doors swing open.

III

The Linen Ceremony

Bedding is laid with geometric precision—stepped folds mirroring the train's Art Deco lines. Each pillow is placed at a 45-degree angle, a signature of the house.

IV

The Dawn Toast

As the first light reaches Meridian Crest, the conductor raises a glass of chilled vermouth to each guest. The toast: "To the coast that carries us home."

V

The Luggage Seal

Every piece of luggage receives a wax-sealed label bearing the Aurelian crest and the passenger's initials. It is returned at the terminus, unbroken and intact.
