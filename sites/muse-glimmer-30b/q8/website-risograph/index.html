FAQ

Is it accessible?

Yes. Step-free entry, quiet hours 10–12, high-contrast signage, and a printed schedule in large type.

Can I bring my own paper?

Workshops provide paper. For the open fair, please don’t bring loose scraps — we’ll have a collage bin.

Do I need to book?

Fair entry is free. Workshops are pay-what-you-can with limited spots — sign up via the newsletter.

Will posters ship?

Yes. UK shipping included for orders over £25. International on request.
