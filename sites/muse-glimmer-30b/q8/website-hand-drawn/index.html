Featured Observation

The night the fungi lit the log

October 12, after rain. I returned to the fallen maple I’d been watching for weeks. In the dark, the log was a map of foxfire — Armillaria maybe, or a shy glow. I didn’t photograph it. I sat, wrote, and let the light come and go with my breath. The air smelled of earth and cold iron. A vole rustled. I left the log alone.

Note to self: return with red light only.
