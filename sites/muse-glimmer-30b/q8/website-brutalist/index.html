SALTWALL DESALINATION PLANT
POINT VERDE

[GRID 07/09 • COASTAL • 1968-1974]

Monumental concrete intake walls form a brutalist breakwater. The plant is a civic machine, not a building. Water enters through 12 meter shafts, is forced through ceramic filters, and is discharged as potable. The structure is exposed to salt, wind, and public neglect — and it holds.

Site plans show the original intention: the plant as a civic plaza, with public ramps leading to the sea wall. The ramps were never completed. The concrete remains, raw, measured, confrontational.
