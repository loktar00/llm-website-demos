Collection Introduction

The Archive documents the development of transit information design from 1900 to present. Objects are selected for typographic rigour, diagrammatic clarity and systemic coherence. The collection emphasises Swiss International Style principles: grid, hierarchy, neutrality and precision.

All items are catalogued with original dimensions, material, typeface, colour specification and context of use. No reproductions are used in public display.

Featured Designer Essay

Massimo Vignelli — The Grid as Public Service

For Vignelli, transit information was not decoration but civic infrastructure. The 1972 New York City Subway map reduced complexity to a regular lattice, privileging legibility over geography. Type was set in Helvetica 55 Roman at 10pt with 14pt leading. Colour was limited to black, white and a single red accent for critical information.

“Design is not a product; it is a process.”

The archive holds original paste-ups, colour proofs and installation photographs. The work demonstrates controlled asymmetry through alignment to a 12-column grid, generous white space, and the disciplined use of red.
