Featured observation

The Standing Oak • 22 November

Light slanted through beech, caught the oak’s trunk in amber. Bark plates like old paper. A woodpecker worked a rhythm, tap tap pause. I sat on a log for an hour, watching ants ferry crumbs up a fissure. The air smelled of leaf mold and distant woodsmoke. I sketched the crown three times, each less accurate, more true. A crow called once, then silence.

I think the tree is listening.

About the naturalist

I walk the same woods most weeks, different light each time. I’m not an expert, just attentive. This book is for remembering the small things that would otherwise slip past: the way moss holds a footprint, the exact green after rain.
