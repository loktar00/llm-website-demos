Watch the layers land

Every riso print is a small weather system: drums of translucent ink passing over paper, one colour at a time, never quite agreeing with each other. Watch our four separations cycle — that wobble is the charm.

Before you ask

Fair questions

Do I need a ticket?

Entry to the fair is free — just turn up. Workshops have limited seats and can be reserved from Monday 1 June via the newsletter link we'll send out. Live printing sessions are first-come, first-inked.

I've never printed anything. Is this for me?

Especially for you. Riso 101 assumes zero experience and maximum curiosity. Half our printers started by accidentally wandering into this exact fair in a previous year and never recovering.

Can I bring my own zines to sell or swap?

Yes! The swap table is open all weekend, free of charge. Ten casual stall spots for small presses are released each morning at 9:30 at the info desk — cash box, honour system, good vibes.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free access to the Main Hall, Print Wall and Zine Garden; a lift serves the Talk Loft and Workshop Barn. Accessible toilets on both floors. Quiet hour on Sunday 10:00–11:00. Email access@inkweather.example for anything we've missed.

Why is everything slightly off-register?

That's not a bug, that's the weather. Riso drums are mechanical, moody, and beautiful; every sheet comes out a little different. We consider perfect registration a missed opportunity.
