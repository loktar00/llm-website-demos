# AK-47 field strip

A scroll-driven 3D showcase of an AK-47 being field stripped. The rifle floats in a grey
studio and fires on a loop: the bolt carrier cycles, the shot drops into slow motion, a muzzle
flash lights the receiver, heat shimmers off the barrel and the brass tumbles, bounces and
settles. Scroll and it comes apart in manual order (magazine, dust cover, recoil spring, gas
tube, bolt carrier, bolt), ending in an exploded view with diagram callouts. Drag to rotate,
pinch or +/- to zoom; sound is synthesized in the page and off until you turn it on.

Every part is a TRELLIS.2 image-to-3D model built from a single generated image (Krea2 and
Qwen Image 2.1 on ComfyUI), fitted to real-world dimensions and assembled in code. By Claude
Opus 5.5.

Single `index.html` (three.js from a CDN) with the seven `.glb` models beside it, no build step.
