A

Access for every rider

Step-free at 58 of 64 stations

Lifts at every hub

Platform-to-concourse lifts at all 14 interchange stations and 44 other stops. Lift status is live on station boards and this site.

Step-free routes

Route-finder shows step-free journeys only when you ask. Six stations are ramp-only; all others offer a lift or assisted path.

Audio & tactile guidance

Every platform has tactile paving, audio announcements on both tracks, and a tactile map board at the fare gate.

Assisted boarding

Reserve assistance up to 24 h ahead or walk in up to 10 min before your train. Our team meets you at the lift, not the gate.

Clear wayfinding

Bold high-contrast signage, braille on every board, and pictograms first — words second — across the network.

Real-time information

Low platforms at 12 stations; gap-filler boards on every train. All displays include audio and large-text modes.
